New `cclens jobs`: scheduled tasks (grouped by the task's name) and sessions you repeat by hand (same title, three or more runs) shown as jobs, with runs, cost per run, how often they run and the expected monthly cost. Jobs that look stopped and one-off runs are not projected forward. `cclens jobs --job <name>` lists one job's runs. `cclens review` gains a `recurring-cost` rule that points at the costliest jobs and how to make each run cheaper.
